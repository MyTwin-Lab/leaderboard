## Context

Someone who wants to contribute has to guess where to start.

They land on a challenge, read a title and a paragraph, and the next thing the page offers is a workspace that assumes they already decided. Nothing tells them what the work is, what it pays, who else is on it, or what happens the minute they click join.

This challenge rebuilds that first hour: what a contributor reads before joining, what they get on the board when they do, and how they know where they stand afterwards.

## Objective

What has to be shipped:

- A challenge page that answers the questions asked before joining — the problem, the deliverable, the reward, the people.
- A board provisioned on join, carrying the template tasks and the branch, with nothing left to set up by hand.
- A visible state of progress: what is done, what is being evaluated, what has been credited.

## Expected result

- A pull request from your branch, with the board reflecting what it contains.
- The path walked end to end by someone who has never used the Lab, and the notes they took while doing it.
