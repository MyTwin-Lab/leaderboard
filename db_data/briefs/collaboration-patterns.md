## Context

The ledger credits commits. It says nothing about the review that made them land.

Most of what holds a project together leaves no artefact to count: the answer in a thread that unblocked someone, the pull request read carefully on a Friday evening, the person who onboards every newcomer. Counting only what ships rewards the contributor who works alone.

This challenge looks for those patterns in the traces they do leave — reviews, discussion threads, co-authored work — and turns the ones that hold up into something the ledger can pay.

## Objective

What has to be shipped:

- Detection of collaboration signals from the sources already connected: reviews, threads, co-authorship.
- A rule that turns a signal into contribution points, with a cap that keeps it from being farmed.
- A read of the result on real history: who the signals surface, and whether it matches what the team would have said.

## Expected result

- A pull request from your branch, with the board reflecting what it contains.
- A short report on the detected patterns, including the ones you chose not to reward and why.
