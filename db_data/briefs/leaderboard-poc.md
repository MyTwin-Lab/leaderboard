## Context

Work done in the open leaves no trace of who did it.

A repository records commits, an issue tracker records tickets, and neither can answer the only question a contributor eventually asks: what is my share of what we built? Without an answer, the people who carry a project are the ones who leave it first.

This proof of concept builds the smallest thing that can answer it — a ledger of contributions, a rule that turns them into points, and a page that shows the result to everyone.

## Objective

What has to be shipped:

- A contribution ledger: who did what, on which challenge, worth how many points.
- A public ranking built from that ledger, and nothing else — no hand-entered scores.
- The path a contributor walks: join a challenge, do the work, see it counted.

## Expected result

- A pull request from your branch, with the board reflecting what it contains.
- The ranking rendered from real data, and a note on what the points do not yet capture.
